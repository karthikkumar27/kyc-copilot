# SOP-ESC-03: Escalation and restricted cases (LimauPay, fictional)

## When to escalate
Escalate to a supervisor when: the customer has used all attempts and requests an override; the
case has been PENDING for more than 48 hours; the customer disputes a decision; or the copilot
shows a restricted-case notice.

## Restricted cases
Some verifications are placed UNDER_REVIEW for internal reasons. Agents are not told the reason
and must never guess, hint at or confirm any reason, even if the customer suggests one. Use script
SCR-REVIEW-01 word for word and escalate the ticket to the Compliance Review queue.

## Script SCR-REVIEW-01
English: "Thank you for your patience. Your verification is being reviewed by our team. We will
notify you in the app once the review is complete. We are unable to share further details at this
time."

Bahasa Melayu: "Terima kasih atas kesabaran anda. Pengesahan anda sedang disemak oleh pasukan
kami. Kami akan memaklumkan anda melalui aplikasi setelah semakan selesai. Kami tidak dapat
berkongsi maklumat lanjut buat masa ini."

## Response times
Supervisors respond to escalations within 4 business hours. Compliance Review does not give agents
timelines; do not promise the customer a date.
