# SOP-KYC-02: Verification tiers and wallet limits (LimauPay, fictional)

## Tiers
LimauPay has two tiers. BASIC accounts have verified a phone number only. FULL accounts have
completed e-KYC with an accepted identity document.

## BASIC tier limits
Wallet balance limit: RM 200. Monthly incoming transfer limit: RM 1,000. BASIC customers cannot
send money to other wallets or bank accounts and cannot use the overseas QR payment feature.

## FULL tier limits
Wallet balance limit: RM 5,000. Monthly incoming transfer limit: RM 50,000. Transfers to banks and
other wallets are allowed. Overseas QR payments are allowed in supported countries.

## Upgrading
Customers upgrade from BASIC to FULL by completing e-KYC in the app under Profile > Verify
Identity. Upgrades usually complete within 10 minutes. If a case is PENDING for more than 48 hours,
the agent should raise a ticket to the KYC Operations queue with the customer reference (CUS-
number), never the IC number.

## Funds while unverified
If a BASIC customer receives money that would exceed the balance limit, the excess transfer is
rejected and returned to the sender within 3 business days. Agents should explain this and suggest
completing e-KYC.
