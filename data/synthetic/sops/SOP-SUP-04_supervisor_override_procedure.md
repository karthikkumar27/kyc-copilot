# SOP-SUP-04: Supervisor override procedure (LimauPay, fictional, supervisors only)

## Attempt Override
A supervisor may grant one additional verification attempt per customer per calendar month.
Before granting it, check that the previous failures were for disclosable reasons (for example
DOC_BLURRY or LIVENESS_FAILED). Never grant an override on a case that is UNDER_REVIEW.

## Approval and logging
Every override needs a written reason of at least 20 characters. Overrides are logged with the
supervisor ID and reviewed weekly by KYC Operations. More than 10 overrides by one supervisor in a
week triggers a review.

## Repeated failures
If a customer fails liveness 3 times across 2 separate days, do not grant an override. Raise the
case to KYC Operations for manual review instead.
